# Cardio Care Suite

Bedside planners for cardiovascular care by Warodom Phaenthong, M.D. Each app is one self-contained HTML file
that runs in the browser, with no installation and no data sent anywhere.

They are decision support for healthcare professionals, not a substitute for guidelines, product labels or clinical judgement.

## Apps

- `af-care-suite.html`: all six planners in one page, switched by tabs. `index.html` opens it.
- `af-care-planner.html`: AF-CARE plan (ESC 2024 AF): work-up, rate or rhythm control, anticoagulation by CHA₂DS₂-VA and HAS-BLED.
- `hf-care-planner.html`: heart failure phenotype, stage and medication titration.
- `htn-care-planner.html`: hypertension titration to guideline targets (ESC 2024 / AHA-ACC 2025) and secondary-cause work-up.
- `periop-cv-planner.html`: peri-operative cardiovascular assessment.
- `doac-planner.html`: DOAC choice, dose and dose-reduction criteria for the patient.
- `warfarin-planner.html`: warfarin dose adjustment (Thai Heart Association 2010 nomogram), a weekly tablet schedule, and a TTR calculator.

Each app has a changelog: tap the version badge next to its title.

## Source

The standalone planners are the source. `af-care-suite.html` is generated from them.
