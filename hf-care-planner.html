Based on the 2026 ESC Guidelines for the management of heart failure (Eur Heart J 2026; doi:10.1093/eurheartj/ehag100). New nomenclature: FMT foundational medical therapy (HFrEF: beta-blocker, ACE-I/ARNI/ARB, MRA, SGLT2-I · HFpEF: MRA, SGLT2-I), AMT additional medical therapy, GDIT guideline-directed interventional therapy. HFmrEF is retired: HFrEF = LVEF <50%, HFpEF = LVEF ≥50%. eGFR by CKD-EPI 2021 (race-free). Items marked “practical” are common practice or product labelling, not a guideline recommendation.
